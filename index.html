The program behind ENA

LEAPS Women Economic Empowerment Program

Our Story

Every stitch opens a new beginning.

The LEAPS Women Economic Empowerment Program, through ENA (Eastern Narratives), trains women from underserved communities in textile and leather product making. The work creates pathways to financial independence and a better future.

Every crafted product narrates a story of heritage, resilience and new beginnings.

Skills

Training in hand embroidery and leather product making.

Income

Every order is paid work, and a step towards financial independence.

Heritage

Traditional motifs, carried into products for today's buyers.

When you buy from ENA in volume, you're commissioning work from the women of the program.
