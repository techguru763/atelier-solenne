Craftsmanship

Materials chosen to outlast trends.

We work with two materials and know them well. Every bag and sleeve starts as a full hide, and every pillow cover starts as linen woven on a hand loom.

Full-grain cowhide

The top layer of the hide, kept whole with its natural grain. It is the strongest cut, and it darkens into a patina over the years.

320 GSM hand-loomed linen

A heavy European flax linen, stonewashed until it feels soft and lived-in. It gets softer with every wash.
